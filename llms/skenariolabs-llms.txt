# Skenariolabs

> The data backbone of real estate finance. Auditable asset-level intelligence for European financial institutions.

Skenariolabs provides automated, data-driven intelligence for real estate finance. We serve 200+ institutional clients across Europe, covering over 60 billion euros of collateral under active monitoring.

## What we do

We turn fragmented real estate data into decision-ready, auditable intelligence at the asset level, at scale. From a single address, we reconstruct complete building profiles covering valuation, climate risk, energy performance, technical condition, and renovation potential.

## Platform

- [Automated Valuation](/platform/automated-valuation): Asset-specific market values and rent estimates with monthly forecasts and stress testing.
- [Climate Risk](/platform/climate-risk): Physical and transition risk, energy performance, carbon intensity, and stranding year — aligned with CRREM, EU Taxonomy, and ECB guidelines.
- [Technical Risk](/platform/technical-risk): Building condition, renovation backlog, retrofit cost modeling, and value uplift projections.
- [Data Enrichment](/platform/data-enrichment): Complete asset-specific profiles reconstructed from fragmented sources with confidence scoring.
- [Technology](/platform/technology): Purpose-built models trained on European real estate fundamentals with full audit trails.

## Solutions

- [Portfolio Audit](/solutions/portfolio-audit): One-time or periodic deep enrichment of entire portfolios. Audit-ready outputs aligned with EBA, CRR3, EU Taxonomy, MEPS, CRREM, and SFDR.
- [Risk Monitoring](/solutions/risk-monitoring): Continuous monthly tracking of all risk dimensions at the asset level with automated alerts.
- [Loan Origination](/solutions/loan-origination): All capabilities delivered ad hoc into financing and underwriting workflows. From address to decision package, same day.
